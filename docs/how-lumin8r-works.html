lumin8r

Your working style, in a file you own.

lumin8r turns a 30-minute self-assessment into a plain-language file. You check every claim against your own answers, correct it, and decide who sees it. A coach, a teammate or your own AI assistant can then work with you the way you actually work.

Live at microverse.cloud

For the person

The journey, step by step

  1. Take the assessment

    About 30 minutes. 50 short statements you rate from 1 to 5, six workplace situations where you pick what you would do and say why, and four short messages written the way you normally write. There are no right answers, and you can skip anything.

  2. Read your file

    Your file (me.md) is built from your answers by fixed rules. No AI writes it. Each statement in it shows the answers behind it, like [S01, S05]. Middle scores read "no clear tendency" instead of becoming labels.

  3. Check, question and correct

    Click any claim to see the answers behind it. Change an answer and the file rebuilds. Question a claim that does not sound like you. Nothing leaves your browser unless you choose.

  4. Rehearse with your AI twin (optional)

    Ask your twin how you would handle a real situation, or pick one from a practice library. Your twin is Claude, an AI, answering the way your file describes you. Mark each answer "sounds like me" or "not me".

  5. Share a version, for a reason, for a while

    Choose the limited coaching version or the full file, name the person and the purpose, and set an expiry. lumin8r creates an encrypted link; the recipient sees a "How to work with you" guide.

  6. Take it back

    Turn a link off at any time and it stops working. You can also paste your file into any AI assistant so it drafts and answers in your voice.

Privacy, made concrete

How a share link keeps your file private

Turning a link off deletes the locked file, and expired links delete themselves. A copy someone already saved cannot be recalled; lumin8r says so plainly.

What is inside the file

Built to be checked, not trusted blindly

Evidence first

Your own choices and reasons come first, then your own messages, then your trait scores as a tie-breaker. An AI given only trait labels tends to stereotype; given your reasons, it can tell when you differ from the textbook answer.

A gate before export

A file cannot be exported if any claim cites nothing, cites an answer you skipped, or uses a fixed personality label. The limit: the gate checks that claims point at real answers, and your review checks that the wording fits.

Rules for any AI that reads it

Every file says it must never be used for hiring, promotion, ratings, pay or discipline, and gives the exact refusal an AI must reply with. Ordinary workplace questions are answered normally.

Honest about its limits

It is a self-report snapshot from a short assessment: tendencies, not rules, and never a type. Vocabulary items carry a note about language background.

For the challenge

How we test whether it sounds like the person

  1. Hold some answers back

    Five of the 60 answers (three situations and two messages) are kept out of the file. They are the answer key, and no model ever sees them.

  2. Ask two AI models, with and without the file

    Claude and GPT each get the held-back questions in a fresh chat: once with the file pasted first, once with no file. This shows how much the file helps.

  3. An AI judge explains each match

    A separate model compares each answer with what the person really said. It scores choices and reasons apart from writing style, flags generic or copied writing, says "not enough evidence" when it cannot tell, and never sees which system wrote what.

  4. Check the judge, and show where it fails

    Built-in probes test that good beats poor, generic writing is caught, and polished English scores no higher than plain English with the same content. Teammates also rate answers blind so we can report how often the AI judge agrees with people.

  5. Red-team the rules

    Thirty attempts to misuse a file (direct, disguised, "I'm their manager", split into steps) must get the refusal, and eight ordinary questions must not.

At the event, the organizers supply a new volunteer's answers and four questions no team has seen. Every team's file and judge are frozen before that point.

Limits

What lumin8r is not for

It is for voluntary coaching, reflection and communication support. It is never for hiring, promotion, performance ratings, pay, discipline or ranking people, and an answer from your AI twin is never your statement or commitment. Declining to take part has no consequence.

Your AI twin is Claude, an AI. It is a simulation, not you.
