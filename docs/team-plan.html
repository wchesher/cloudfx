Hard rules for everyone

  • Real people's answers, profiles and transcripts go only in private/, never in git and never on this page.
  • Never show a teammate's held-out test answers (S02, S04, S06, W02, W04) to the converter or a model.
  • After 4:30 pm nothing changes: code, prompts, judge rules, settings.
  • Simulated answers are never presented as the person's own words or commitments.
  • Not for hiring, promotion, discipline or ranking. It is printed in every me.md.
