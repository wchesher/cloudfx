Create your personal workplace profile

About this activity

This optional activity creates a personalized description of your workplace preferences, communication style, and how you might approach different situations. It is built from your answers by fixed rules; no AI service writes it.

You will answer up to 60 questions, which should take approximately 20 to 30 minutes. If you took the kit already, bring its answer file.

Your answers will be used to create a personal profile that you can read, question, and correct. The purpose is to support self-reflection, workplace communication, and coaching.

Important things to know

  • Participation is your choice. You may skip questions or decline to participate without penalty or disadvantage. A manager's invitation does not mean you are required to participate.
  • Your profile may not be entirely accurate. It is built from your answers by fixed rules, and those rules can misinterpret answers or overlook important aspects of your experiences, background, or communication style. An AI that answers as you from the profile can get it wrong too.
  • Your profile does not speak for you. It is an AI-generated interpretation, not a diagnosis, a formal employment assessment, or a statement you have personally made or endorsed.
  • Your profile is not for employment decisions. It must not be used for hiring, promotion, disciplinary actions, or employee ranking.
  • You control whether to share your profile. You will have an opportunity to review it before deciding whether to share any version of it with others.

How your information will be used

Your answers will be processed to create and evaluate your personal profile. Before deciding whether to participate, please review the following information:

  • AI services used: building your profile uses no AI service. If you choose Try your twin, your file is sent to Anthropic (Claude Opus 5.5). If you send your kit to your team, the team's evaluation runs use Claude Opus 5.5 and OpenAI GPT Sol 6.1, and an Anthropic judge model scores the results. The optional voice interview uses ElevenLabs; it is switched off today.
  • Who can access your information: on this page, only you: your answers and profile stay in your browser, and the server keeps no answers and no profile, only a record of your consent. In the team hub, the three team members who hold the team code can see the kits and evaluation results sent there.
  • Where your information is stored: in your browser; on the microverse.cloud server, behind the team code, for kits sent to the team hub and the team's evaluation results; and at the AI providers above, for the requests sent to them, under each provider's own retention terms.
  • How long information is kept: until the team deletes it. The team plans to delete teammates' kits and evaluation results after judging. The consent record is kept as the record of what you agreed to.
  • How to withdraw: press Withdraw consent on this page at any time; it revokes your consent and records the withdrawal. Ask the team to delete your kit from the team hub. A file you already downloaded, or pasted into an AI model, cannot be recalled after you withdraw; withdrawal stops further processing here.

After you complete the questionnaire

Before sharing your profile with anyone else, you will have an opportunity to:

  1. Review what the profile says about you and see which answers support its conclusions.
  2. Question or correct information that seems inaccurate or misleading.
  3. Choose whether to share your profile, which version to share, and with whom.
