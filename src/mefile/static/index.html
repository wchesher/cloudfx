1 consent

Your file, your call.

lumin8r turns your Common Kit answers into a me.md file that you read, correct and choose to share.

What it is for: coaching, reflection and communication support. Not hiring, promotion, discipline or ranking.

Who sees your answers: you. Your answers stay in this browser. The server keeps only your consent.

AI services: the file is built without AI. If you paste it into an AI model (Claude, GPT), ask player two to try your twin, or use the voice interview (ElevenLabs), that service processes it under its own terms.

Your control: skip questions, correct answers, withhold whole sections, withdraw. A manager's request does not replace your agreement, and saying no is never recorded as a trait.
