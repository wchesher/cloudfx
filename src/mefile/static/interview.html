A short interview about real situations at work. Your answers, in your words, become examples your file can point to.

8, each "tell me about a time"
Time

Kept

What happens to what you say

  • Only words are used. Nothing about your tone of voice, pace or emotion is analyzed. Your voice is never copied, and no audio is kept.
  • You check every answer first. Before anything is used you read each answer, fix any wording, and remove anything you would rather leave out.
  • Skip anything. You can skip a question or stop at any time. Leaving this out has no effect on the rest of your file.

Who processes what, for each way

  • Talking with the AI interviewerElevenLabs runs the AI interviewer and turns the conversation into text. Recording is switched off, and the conversation is deleted from ElevenLabs once your transcript is saved.
  • Bringing a Plaud recordingYour recording stays in your Plaud account. You bring only the text transcript; this server reads it to find your lines. Only the answers you keep after review are saved with your progress.
  • Typing your answersOnly the text of your answers is used. It is saved with your progress and joins your file only when you choose.
