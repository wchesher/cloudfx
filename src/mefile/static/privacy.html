privacy

Your file stays yours

lumin8r builds your file without AI and saves your progress so you never lose it. You decide who gets your file, and for how long.

Last updated 14 November 2026.

No AI builds your file

Your file is made from your answers by fixed rules.

Saved on the server, not in your browser

Your progress is saved on the lumin8r server under a private cookie and deleted when you finish, close the browser, or leave it for an hour, or when you ask.

Share links we cannot read

Your browser encrypts what you share. The server holds a locked copy and never gets the key.

You can take it back

Turn off a link or withdraw consent at any time. Copies someone already made cannot be recalled.

What is processed, and when

  • Saved progress. As you go, your answers, interview, notes and choices are saved on the lumin8r server under a random ID in a private cookie (HttpOnly; it holds no content). Nothing is kept in your browser's storage. Nothing carries over between sessions.
  • Building your file. Your answers are converted on the lumin8r server by fixed rules. No AI is used.
  • Sharing (optional). When you create a share link, your browser encrypts the version you chose with a fresh key (AES-GCM). The server receives only the encrypted copy, plus the purpose, the recipient label, the version and the expiry date you typed. The key is in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to servers, so the server cannot read what you shared.
  • Rehearse with your understudy (optional). Only when you ask your understudy something, your file (me.md) and your question go through our server to Anthropic (Claude), which writes the answer. lumin8r does not store them; Anthropic processes them under its API terms.
  • Voice interview (when it is turned on). The interview runs with ElevenLabs, and only the text transcript is used. It is off unless the operator turns it on, and you choose whether to take it.
  • Consent events. The server records that you consented, created a share, revoked a share, or withdrew. The consent record holds events, never your answers or your file.

Who can see it

  • You. Your saved progress opens only in a browser holding your cookie or your continue link.
  • The people you share with. Anyone holding the whole link, key included, can open that shared version until it expires or you turn it off. Send it only to the person it is for.
  • The operator of this lumin8r service. They can see the consent record: when you consented, and for each share its purpose, recipient label, version and dates. Saved progress is stored on their server, so they can technically reach it; they do not browse it. They cannot read what you shared.
  • AI providers. Anthropic sees what you send to your understudy. ElevenLabs sees your voice interview, if you take one.

Nobody else can open your saved progress through lumin8r without your cookie or your continue link.

Where it is kept

  • Your browser: only a cookie holding a random ID. No answers and no file.
  • The lumin8r server: your saved progress; encrypted share copies with their purpose, recipient label, version and dates; and the consent record.
  • Anthropic and ElevenLabs: the requests you send them, under each provider's own retention terms.

How long it is kept

  • Saved progress: until you finish, close the browser, or leave it for an hour, then deleted. "Forget this device" in Your file, or "Clear my answers" in the assessment, deletes it at once; "Withdraw consent" deletes your answers, file and interview.
  • Share links: until the expiry date you chose (at most 90 days) or until you turn the link off, whichever comes first. Then the encrypted copy is deleted and the link stops working.
  • The consent record: kept as the record of what you agreed to and when. It holds events, never content.
  • AI providers: as their own terms say.

Your rights

  • Review. Every claim in your file shows the answers behind it.
  • Correct. Change any answer and your file rebuilds.
  • Question. Mark any claim you think is wrong.
  • Revoke. Turn off any share link in Your file. The server copy is deleted at once.
  • Withdraw. "Withdraw consent" in Your file ends your consent and records the withdrawal. Nothing more is processed for you after that.
  • Say no. Taking the assessment and sharing are voluntary. Saying no has no consequence.

What cannot be undone

Turning off a link stops the link, not copies. If someone saved, printed or copied what you shared, or you downloaded your me.md or pasted it into an AI tool, lumin8r cannot get those copies back. Withdrawal stops processing here; it cannot reach copies elsewhere.
