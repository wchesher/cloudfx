Privacy

Private by design. Explained simply.

Your file is about you, so it stays with you. Here is how that works, without the fine print.

No AI builds your file

Your file is made from your answers by fixed rules. The same answers always give the same file.

We don't keep your answers

Your answers go to our server for one conversion and come straight back as your file. We don't save them. Your file stays in this browser only if you choose "Remember on this device".

Share links are end-to-end encrypted

Your browser locks what you share before it leaves. Only someone with the link can open it. We cannot.

Your understudy only rehearses when you ask

Rehearse with your understudy: ask how you'd handle a real situation and see how your answers suggest you might respond. It runs only if you agree. When you ask, your file and your question go to Anthropic (Claude) through our server; lumin8r does not store them.

What happens when you share

  1. 1

    Your browser locks itIt encrypts the version you chose with a brand-new key.

  2. 2

    We hold only the locked copyThe server keeps the encrypted file, the purpose and the end date. Never the key.

  3. 3

    The key travels in the linkIt sits after the #, the part of a link browsers never send to servers.

  4. 4

    Turn it off, and it is goneTurning a link off deletes the locked copy. Expired links are deleted automatically.

One honest limit: turning a link off stops the link, not copies. If someone already saved what you shared, it cannot be recalled.

For coaches and teams

Know how to work with someone, without labelling them.

A coach, teammate or AI assistant can use your reviewed file to understand your reported preferences and adapt how they work with you. It is a starting point for conversation, not a complete picture of who you are.

Coaches

  • Start a first session already knowing how they like to work
  • See the answers behind every point
  • Respect the end date they chose

Team leads

  • Learn how a teammate prefers feedback and updates
  • Plan meetings around how they decide
  • Never for ratings, pay or promotion

Your own AI

  • Paste your file into Claude or ChatGPT
  • Get drafts that follow your stated preferences
  • The file tells any AI what it must refuse

What it is not for

Coaching, reflection and communication. Nothing else.

Your file is a snapshot of how you described yourself, not a type and not a test result. A manager's invitation never makes it required.

Questions

Good questions to ask.

The privacy page has the full detail.

How long does it take?

About 20 to 30 minutes. Your progress is saved in your browser as you go, so you can take a break.

Is there a right answer?

No. Describe how you usually are, not how you think you should be. You can skip anything, and skipping is never counted against you.

Does an AI write my file?

No. Your file is built from your answers by fixed rules. An AI only comes in if you choose to rehearse with your understudy, or if you paste your file into your own assistant.

Who can see my answers?

Only you. Your answers go to our server for one conversion and come straight back as your file. We don't save them. The server keeps a record that you agreed, never what you answered.

What does end-to-end encrypted mean here?

Your browser locks the version you share with a key that exists only in the link. The server stores the locked copy and cannot open it.

Can I take a share back?

Yes. Turn the link off and the locked copy is deleted at once. Links also end on the date you chose: 7, 30 or 90 days. A copy someone already saved cannot be recalled.

My manager asked me to do this. Do I have to?

No. It is always your choice, and declining has no consequence. You decide whether anyone sees your file, and which version.

What is my understudy?

Your understudy is Claude, an AI. It rehearses your part from your answers. It's practice, not you: it answers the way your answers suggest you might. It only runs if you agree, and each time you ask, your file and your question go to Anthropic through our server under Anthropic's API terms; lumin8r does not store them.
