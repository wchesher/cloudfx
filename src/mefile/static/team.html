3 · Judge checks

Check the judge against human ratings

Two reviewers rate the same responses blind, then compare their ratings with the judge's.

step 1 · rating sheet

The sheet hides which model and which condition wrote each response. Send the link to your raters; they rate on the page and press Save to the team.

step 2 · ratings

Raters press Save to the team on the sheet; their ratings appear here. No files to pass around.

Rating files

Judge self-check

Does the judge separate strong responses from weak ones?

Ten fixed responses for the fictional DEMO01 person: ones that match, ones that contradict, corporate filler, and the same content in polished and in plain English. Each probe says what a good judge should do, and whether this one did.

Failures are evidence: the challenge asks for at least one.

4 · Results

Score the runs

Score checks scenario choices against the held-out answers, without a model. Score and judge also asks the judge how closely each response resembles the person.
